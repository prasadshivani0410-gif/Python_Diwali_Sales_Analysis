# Diwali Sales Analysis 🪔

A simple exploratory data analysis (EDA) project to understand customer buying patterns during Diwali sales. The goal is to find out **who is buying, what they are buying, and from where**.

---

## What This Project Does

This notebook digs into Diwali sales data and answers questions like:
- Which gender buys more?
- Which age group spends the most?
- Which states have the highest orders and sales?
- Does marital status affect buying behavior?
- What occupations do most buyers have?
- Which product categories are most popular?

---

## Dataset

The dataset used is `Diwali Sales Data.csv`. It contains customer information and purchase details collected during a Diwali sale event.

---

## Libraries Used

- **Pandas** – for data loading and manipulation
- **NumPy** – for numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** – for plotting charts
- **Seaborn** – for better-looking visualizations
   

1.. Install the required libraries
   ```bash
   pip install numpy pandas matplotlib seaborn
   ```

2. Place the `Diwali Sales Data.csv` file in the same folder

3. Open the notebook
   ```bash
   jupyter notebook Diwali_Sales_Analysis.ipynb
   ```

---

## Key Findings

- **Gender** – Most buyers are female, and they also spend more than males
- **Age Group** – Women between **26–35 years** are the biggest buyers
- **States** – Top orders and sales come from **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka**
- **Marital Status** – Married women have the highest purchasing power
- **Occupation** – Most buyers work in **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation**
- **Product Category** – **Food, Clothing, and Electronics** are the top-selling categories

---

## Conclusion

> Married women aged 26–35 from UP, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, working in IT, Healthcare, or Aviation, are most likely to buy Food, Clothing, and Electronics during Diwali sales.
